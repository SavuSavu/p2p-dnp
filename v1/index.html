HOST · 5 STEPS

  1. Copy the invite link into your chat.
  2. Create the peer offer.
  3. Copy the offer into the same chat.
  4. Paste and apply the guest answer.
  5. Wait for “connected,” then play.

GUEST · 5 STEPS

  1. Open the host’s invite link.
  2. Paste and apply the host offer from chat.
  3. Copy your generated answer.
  4. Send that answer back through chat.
  5. Wait for “connected,” then play.

DO THIS NOW · DIRECT CONNECTION
